How to Play Rummy

🎯 Objective

Be the first to meld all your cards into valid groups (sets or runs) and go out!

🃏 Variants

Classic Rummy

7 cards each, 1 deck, 2–6 players. Great for beginners.

Gin Rummy

10 cards each, 1 deck, 2–6 players. More strategy, classic style.

Indian Rummy

13 cards each, 2 decks, 2–6 players. The most popular variant in India.

🔄 On Your Turn

  • Draw — pick from the face-down deck OR the top discard card
  • Arrange — select cards and click "Add Meld" to group them
  • Discard — select one card and click "Discard Selected" to end your turn
  • Go Out — when all cards form valid melds (one card left to discard), click "Declare & Go Out"!

✅ Valid Melds

  • Set — 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, different suits (e.g. 7♦ 7♥ 7♣)
  • Run — 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g. 4♠ 5♠ 6♠ 7♠)
  • Ace can be low (A-2-3) in Classic/Gin

📊 Scoring Modes

  • Loser Pays — each round, losers score points equal to unmelded card values. Low score wins.
  • First to 100 Loses — accumulate points each round; first player to hit 100 is out. Last one standing wins.
  • Race to Target — winner earns opponents' deadwood points. First to reach the target wins.

🃏 Card Points

A, J, Q, K = 10 pts each  ·  Number cards = face value

💡 Tips

  • Draw from the discard pile only if it completes a meld
  • Discard high-point cards early if they don't fit your melds
  • Watch the discard pile — it tells you what opponents need!
